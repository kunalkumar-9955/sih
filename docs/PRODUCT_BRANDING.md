# RouteIQ — Product Branding Guide

---

## Product Name

**RouteIQ**

## Product Description

Smart Multi-Modal Logistics & Risk Intelligence Platform

## SIH Problem Statement

**SIH26002** — Ministry of Development of North Eastern Region (MDoNER)

---

> **IMPORTANT:** SIH26002 is the **SIH problem statement identifier** and the **competition reference number** assigned by Smart India Hackathon. It is NOT the product name, app name, website title, or primary brand.

---

## Branding Hierarchy

PRIMARY:     RouteIQ
SECONDARY:   Smart Multi-Modal Logistics & Risk Intelligence Platform
REFERENCE:   SIH Problem Statement: SIH26002

---

## Rationale for Name Choice

RouteIQ was selected because:
- Route: directly reflects the core function: multimodal route planning across road, rail, and waterway networks
- IQ: conveys intelligence, AI-powered decision support, and risk analytics
- Short and memorable: 7 characters, easy to say, professional
- Relevant to logistics: immediately understood in the freight/transport domain
- Suitable for mobile, web, and SIH demo presentation

---

## What SIH26002 Is (and Is Not)

SIH26002 IS:
- The SIH competition problem statement number
- The government-assigned identifier for this challenge
- A reference number shown in footer/demo sections
- A technical reference in backend docstrings
- A badge shown next to the RouteIQ logo

SIH26002 IS NOT:
- The product name
- The application title
- The dashboard brand
- The browser title
- The primary user-facing brand
